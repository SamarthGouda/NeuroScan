## 2. Genuine Features & Architecture Verified

Every frame of this video was generated by capturing live screen recordings of the running NeuroScan AI system at `http://localhost:8000`:

1. **Clinician Doctor Dashboard (`/doctor-dashboard`)**
   - Active system status indicators, workload metrics, and real-time clinical dispatch.
2. **Multi-Modal MRI Acquisition Dropzone (`/upload`)**
   - Live tensor validation, axial slice inspection, and clinical intake form.
3. **Tumor Classification & Inference (`/scan/:id`)**
   - Real model prediction from `concat_no_attn` with 89.6% Glioma probability.
   - Genuine Bayesian Monte Carlo Dropout uncertainty metric (LOW risk tier).
4. **Attention U-Net Lesion Segmentation (`/scan/:id`)**
   - Pixel-accurate segmentation mask overlaid on real axial brain slice.
   - Lesion area quantification (1.27% cross-sectional area, 638 pixels).
5. **Multi-Method Explainability Suite (XAI)**
   - Side-by-side heatmaps: Grad-CAM, Grad-CAM++, Score-CAM, and Integrated Gradients.
6. **Quantitative Radiomics Biomarkers**
   - 10 computed texture/intensity biomarkers with SHAP feature importance bars.
7. **Multi-Model Ablation Study Benchmarks (`/statistics`)**
   - Verified comparative metrics directly from `saved_models/ablation_summary.json` (Concat No-Attn: 0.99994 AUC, Hybrid: 0.9998 AUC, ResNet-50: 0.9994 AUC, Swin: 0.9992 AUC).
   - Validated fallback mechanism implemented in `backend/inference/predictor.py`.
